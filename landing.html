Why Vendsr? A Powerful Yet Simple Tool for Merchants and Shoppers

Create Your Own Online Store – In Minutes

You don’t need a website. You don’t need a developer. With Vendsr, you can set up a beautiful online store directly from your phone. Upload your product photos, enter the names, prices, and descriptions, and boom, your store is live with a unique link you can share anywhere. Whether you're selling Ankara, pastries, perfumes, or soft drinks, you’ll have a store that’s ready to receive orders 24/7.

Manage Orders Like a Pro

Every order your customers place is tracked in your dashboard. You’ll see what’s pending, what’s paid for, and what’s delivered. You can update the status of orders in real-time so your customers stay informed and confident. Whether it’s one order a day or fifty, you’ll stay organized and in control.

Analytics to Help You Grow

Vendsr doesn’t just help you sell - it helps you grow. Get simple, clear insights into how your business is doing. Which products are selling the most? What days are your customers most active? How much have you earned this month? Our built-in analytics give you all the answers, so you can make smarter decisions without stress.

Friendly Shopping Experience

We didn’t just think of the seller; we also made things easy for the buyer. Your customers don’t need to download an app or create an account. They just click your link, view your products, pay securely, and receive updates. No confusion. No hidden fees. Just smooth, stress-free shopping — the way it should be.

How It Works: A Simple, Secure, and Fast Process for Merchants and Shoppers

For merchants - start selling in 3 simple steps

  • Create your free store

    Sign up in minutes using your phone number or email. Enter your business name, add your store link, and you’re good to go.

  • add your product

    Upload photos, enter names, descriptions, and prices. Add variations like sizes or colors if needed. Everything is clean, simple, and mobile-friendly.

  • share & sell

    Share your Vendsr store link on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or SMS. Once customers order, you get real-time notifications and can manage everything from your dashboard.

For shoppers - shop in 3 simple steps

  • tap the store link

    Click on the seller’s Vendsr link (shared on WhatsApp, IG bio, or anywhere). You don’t need an account to shop.

  • browse and order

    View the products with photos, prices, and clear descriptions. Add items to cart and proceed to checkout with ease.

  • pay securely & relax

    Choose your payment method (card, transfer, USSD). Once you pay, the seller gets your order instantly, and you’ll get updates on your order status.
